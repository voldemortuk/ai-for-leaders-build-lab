The Leaders' Build Lab

Reimagining the hands-on build as a team mini-hackathon — leaders ship real AI agents together, in one room, in one sitting.

⏱️2–2.5 hours · single sitting 👥Teams of 4–5 leaders 🛠️1 Build Coach per team
Session experience walkthrough · for review
Why we're changing the build
The solo build splits the room. A team sprint pulls it together.
▼ Today · Solo hands-on build
Everyone follows one screen, alone
The instructor builds; each leader tries to keep up on their own laptop.
  • Fast builders finish early and coast — it feels too easy
  • Anyone who slips behind quietly disengages — it feels too hard
  • The energy leaks to impatience; the value lands unevenly
  • Leaders leave having watched a build, not owned one
▲ Proposed · Team mini-hackathon
Small teams build toward a live demo
Leaders build in teams, each with an embedded senior engineer, racing to a 3-minute demo.
  • The team absorbs the spread — fast pull, slower learn by doing
  • A Build Coach unblocks instantly; nobody is left falling behind
  • The demo deadline creates energy the solo format never had
  • Every leader leaves with two working agents and a story

How the room works
One instructor up front. Each team has its own senior engineer.
🎤
Front of room
The Instructor
Runs the room, not the keyboards. Sets the scene, runs the stock-vs-custom contrast, holds the clock, calls the check-in, and MCs the demos. One per session.
🛠️
Inside each team
The Build Coach
A senior engineer from a FAANG+ company (our instructor pool), embedded in one team. Doesn't lecture — pairs, unblocks, and keeps the team shipping. One per team.
👥
The builders
The Team
4–5 leaders who build together and present together. Shared screen, shared scenario, one demo at the end. 4–5 teams in a typical leadership cohort.
⚖️
The Build Coach is the engine of the format — it's what lets a mixed-pace room of senior leaders all reach a working demo. The coach absorbs the gap; the instructor protects the pace.
One scenario, every team · No BYOP
Everyone builds for the same executive.
MP
Marko's Day
Marko Petrov — VP Customer Experience & Commercial Operations, Yettel Serbia (synthetic telco scenario).
  • The setup — back-to-back meetings, a fast inbox, four fires competing for his week.
  • The job — each team builds Marko an AI team that hands him his world in minutes.
  • The kit — pre-built Yettel knowledge base + shared workshop accounts. Zero setup.
✦ Standardised — no bring-your-own-project
1
Market Pulse Build · ~35 min
A 5-minute market briefing agent, framed for Yettel. Teaches the core discipline: a good agent asks before it runs.
2
Meeting Prep Build · ~40 min
Walks Marko into his 1:1 informed — reads his calendar, email and Teams, then an "updated read" once it scans.
▶
Monday Morning Brief Demo only
An agent that runs on a schedule via Power Automate. The instructor demos it — the "ceiling" beyond what teams build.
Why standardised: zero setup friction, teams are comparable, the room races on one problem — and the format runs anywhere, cold.
